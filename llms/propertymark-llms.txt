# Propertymark

> Propertymark is the United Kingdom's largest professional body for property agents, formed in 2017 from the merger of the NAEA, ARLA, ICBA and NAVA, and representing over 19,000 members across residential sales, lettings, commercial property, inventories, auctions and valuation. It accredits AGENTS, not listing data: it publishes the Conduct and Membership Rules, operates a government-approved Client Money Protection scheme, runs Propertymark Qualifications as the sector's specialist awarding organisation, and lobbies government on estate and letting agency regulation.

## Machine-readable status

- Propertymark publishes **no API**: no OpenAPI, no AsyncAPI, no GraphQL, no OData `$metadata`, no webhooks, no SDK, no CLI, no Postman collection and no developer portal. Verified by anonymous probe on 2026-07-26.
- Every developer-shaped hostname fails DNS resolution: `developer.`, `developers.`, `api.`, `data.`, `standards.`, `my.`, `portal.`, `members.`, `login.`, `identity.`, `sso.`, `auth.` and `idp.propertymark.co.uk`.
- Every developer-shaped path returns an honest HTTP 404 on `www.propertymark.co.uk`: `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/api`, `/apis`, `/developers`, `/docs`, `/graphql`, `/rest`, `/$metadata`, `/odata`, `/llms.txt`.
- RESO is **absent, not unreachable**. The United Kingdom has no MLS, so there is no listing-data certification layer. Propertymark's own site search returns "result: 0 of 0" for both "RESO" and "OData", and the RESO certification directory contains no Propertymark entry.
- The only machine-readable surface on the estate is an **undocumented** Preside CMS page handler behind the public Find an Expert directory. It is deliberately not listed as an API here — no documentation, no terms, no versioning, no contract. See `review.yml`.
- Genuinely open UK property data comes from HM Land Registry and Ordnance Survey, not from the agents' professional body.
